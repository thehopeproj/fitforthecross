Grace

Grace is the undeserved kindness and power of God working in us and for us.

A word from Coach

Welcome to November! It's officially holiday season and you know what that means — traditions, turkey, and tons of extra things to do. November kinda feels like loading up a heavy barbell for a back squat session. You know the load is about to go up. And when the load of life increases, so does your need for grace.

Which is why grace is your training focus this month. Grace is the undeserved kindness and power of God working in us and for us. It is the first ingredient in the formula for salvation. And if you don't know this about food labels, the first ingredient is always the largest contributor. As you grow in your faith, you realize not only the power of God's grace, but also how essential it is for godly training. And you're going to get plenty of training this month.

But don't rush it. Don't be like the stores who put out Christmas decorations in September. The days in November are still 24 hours long and they still move at the same speed. Stay present in the moment, stack your quality reps with God, and fuel your soul with the spiritual nutrition this month's DAILY FUEL provides.

Quality > Quantity // Presence > Performance

Shalom,
Coach Trey
