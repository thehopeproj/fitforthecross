The Problem Isn't Access. It's Structure.

You have 24/7, uninterrupted access to God. Access has never been the problem.

The problem is that most people treat their spiritual life the way a casual gym-goer treats a treadmill — they show up when they feel like it, do what feels comfortable, and wonder why nothing truly changes. They have access to everything they need. But what they really need is a structured spiritual training program.

When you train your body without structure, you plateau. When you train your soul without structure, you can miss. You can miss the depth of connection God is already making available to you. And when life gets hard — and it will — you can find yourself underprepared for the intensity of it.

Fit for the Cross™ gives your spiritual life the same training structure your fitness life already has.

Train Your Soul Every Day.

At its core, Fit for the Cross™ is all about daily spiritual training — delivered through DAILY FUEL. Drawing on the principles of strength training and the methodology of functional fitness, every DAILY FUEL session is designed to supply the spiritual nourishment you need for the challenges of life.

View a FUEL Session →

Every morning, a FUEL session lands in your inbox. Sessions draw from 22 different spiritual disciplines — including Bible study, prayer, fasting, stillness, journaling, and worship — and our development team ensures you touch all 22 disciplines at least once a month. Most sessions can be completed in 15 minutes or less. You're training with the same spiritual practices as the ancient church.

But we mix it up. Because the same exercise repeated the same way produces the same result — and eventually, little or no result at all. Variety prevents the plateau that can lead to a spiritual rut. Plus it keeps it fresh, keeps it fun, and keeps you from having to figure out what you're going to do.

Each month follows a training focus that shapes everything — the books of the Bible you study, the verses of the day, and the monthly capstone sessions, which build in duration throughout the month. Think 4 minutes of silence in week one, growing to 12 minutes of silence by week four.

That's progressive overload applied to the soul.

Fit for the Cross™ also publishes a weekly email with devotionals, worship playlists, and tips and advice for getting the most out of your time with God, all included in your training — and all for free.

This Is What Spiritual Fitness Looks Like.

If you want to look like a bodybuilder, you need to train like a bodybuilder. The same is true for any sport. So what does it look like to train for godliness?

If you want to look like a follower of Jesus, you need to train like He did. He fasted. He prayed. He went into solitude. He read and memorized Scripture. He worshipped. He did the disciplines.

But what people often miss isn't what He did — it's what it produced. The Bible doesn't describe Jesus' shoulders or quads. It describes His kindness, His faithfulness, His love. Scripture calls these the fruits of the Spirit — the visible evidence of a life trained in godliness.

At Fit for the Cross™, we call them spiritual skills. Not because they're easy. Because they can be trained. And like any great training program, over time they become proof that something real is happening — that the training is working.
